# Data license

The Greek text in `source_text/swete/`, and every file generated from it in
`docs/data/` and `docs/lines/`, derives from the Open Greek and Latin First1KGreek
transcription of H. B. Swete, *The Old Testament in Greek according to the
Septuagint* (tlg0527), © Open Greek and Latin / University of Leipzig, licensed
under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Swete's printed edition is in
the public domain.

Exception: Ecclesiastes and Exod 20:1, Num 17:1, Num 19:1, 3 Kgdms 16:1 (the rows
with `text_source: "Brenton 1851"`) and `source_text/brenton/` are L. C. L.
Brenton's Greek text (London, 1851) as digitized by eBible.org (`grcbrent`), which
is in the public domain. They are included in the same files, which as a whole
remain under CC BY-SA 4.0.

These derived files are shared under the same license. Changes: TEI parsed to
verse text, apparatus and marginal notes removed, OCR artefacts and verse numbers
repaired as documented in `SOURCES.md` and `docs/data/source_repairs.json`, and
word/n-gram indexes computed.
